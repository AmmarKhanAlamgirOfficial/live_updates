The last major U.S.–Russia nuclear arms control treaty expires on Thursday.

It capped nuclear warheads (max 1,550).

It limited delivery systems (max 700).

It ensured transparency (who has what and where).

After this, for the first time since the 1970s, there will be ZERO active nuclear arms reduction treaties between the two biggest nuclear powers.
