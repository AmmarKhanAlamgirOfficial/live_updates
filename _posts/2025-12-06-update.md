Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa:

After liberation, we held a comprehensive national dialogue conference, which led to a provisional constitutional declaration. 

This declaration grants the president the authority to serve for five years, during which we will issue many laws and draft the constitution.

The constitution will serve as the fundamental framework for our system of government. After four years, we will certainly hold elections.
