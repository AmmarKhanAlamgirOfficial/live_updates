Chinese state media has circulated a second exercise poster titled “Arrow of Justice.”

The poster includes the phrases “Arrow of Justice,” “Draw the Sword” (likely referencing earlier Joint Sword drills), and “Counter External Interference.” It also references Strait Thunder-2025A and depicts several green worm-like figures being destroyed.

During April’s exercises, the PLA released imagery portraying Taiwan’s President Lai Ching-te as a green worm.
