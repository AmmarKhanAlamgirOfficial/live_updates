Unverified reports indicate that a substantial $4.6 billion arms agreement was concluded between the Libyan Arab Armed Forces (LAAF) and Pakistan following the visit of Pakistan's senior military commander, General Asim Munir, to Benghazi. 

The alleged package reportedly includes 16 JF-17 Block III fighter jets, 44 Haider main battle tanks, 12 Super Mushshak trainer aircraft, advanced mortar systems, and other military hardware. 

If verified, this deal would mark a major rearmament for the LAAF and a significant strategic export achievement for Pakistan.
