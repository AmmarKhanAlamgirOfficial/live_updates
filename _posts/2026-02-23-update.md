The number of National Guard members killed yesterday rose to 32: 25 in the operation against El Mencho and another 7 in the ensuing violence. 

In Michoacán, Jalisco, and Guanajuato, the deaths of 7 more people, possibly civilians, were reported. 

Meanwhile, 8 criminals have been confirmed killed in the operation against El Mencho, including the drug lord himself.

 At least 13 soldiers have also been confirmed to be injured.
