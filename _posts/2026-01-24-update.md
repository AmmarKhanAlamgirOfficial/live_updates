According to Al-Nahar, Qatar has pledged approximately $480 million to fund the rebuilding of three heavily damaged villages in southern Lebanon. 

In an unusual step aimed at ensuring transparency and oversight, the reconstruction effort will be managed and supervised directly by a U.S. company.
